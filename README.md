# seahawks-player-value-analysis
Excel analysis of nine Seattle Seahawks players comparing 2025 scrimmage yards, touchdowns, and cap hits by position.
# 2025 Seahawks Player Value Analysis

I’m a Seahawks fan, so I wanted to use something I enjoy to practice Excel and build a project for my business analyst portfolio. I looked at nine Seahawks running backs, receivers, and tight ends to see how their 2025 production compared with their cap hits.

[Download my Excel workbook](Seahawks_2025_Player_Value_Analysis.xlsx)

## What I did

I entered each player’s rushing yards, receiving yards, touchdowns, and 2025 cap hit. Then I used Excel formulas to calculate scrimmage yards and production per $1 million of cap hit. I built a PivotTable and chart to compare the three position groups.

## What I found

Of the players I selected, running backs had the most scrimmage yards per $1 million of cap hit at 420.70. Wide receivers had the most total scrimmage yards at 2,835.

## Sources and context
I used the [Seahawks’ 2025 stats](https://www.seahawks.com/team/stats/2025/reg) and [Spotrac’s cap-hit figures](https://www.spotrac.com/nfl/rankings/player/_/year/2025/team/sea/position/off/sort/cap_total). I only included nine players, so this isn’t an analysis of the entire roster. Cap hit also doesn’t tell the whole story of a player’s value.
